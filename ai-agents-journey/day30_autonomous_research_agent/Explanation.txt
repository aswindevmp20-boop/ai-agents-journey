


Until now:

    Agent plans once

    Executes once

    Finishes

Today:

    The agent researches → evaluates → refines → researches again
    until it decides the answer is good enough.

This is how:

    Deep research agents work

    AI analysts operate

    Advanced autonomous systems refine output

Architecture

    User Question
        ↓
    Initial Research
        ↓
    Evaluate answer quality
        ↓
    If incomplete → Refine query
        ↓
    Research again
        ↓
    Final Answer


This introduces:

    Iterative reasoning

    Self-evaluation

    Improvement loop
