Day 58 — Autonomous Research Agent
   Until now your system can:
      • Plan tasks
      • Use tools
      • Execute workflows
      • Maintain memory
      • Retrieve information

   But everything still answers a single query once.

   A research agent does something different:

      It iteratively researches a topic, gathers multiple sources, refines understanding, and produces a structured report.

      Think of it like a mini Perplexity / AI research assistant.

   What the Research Agent Will Do
      User asks:
         "Research the impact of climate change on ocean ecosystems"
         Agent workflow:
         1️⃣ Break topic into subtopics
         2️⃣ Search each topic
         3️⃣ Gather results
         4️⃣ Analyze findings
         5️⃣ Produce structured report
      Example output:
         Title: Impact of Climate Change on Ocean Ecosystems
         Key Areas:
         • Rising sea temperatures
         • Coral bleaching
         • Ocean acidification
         • Marine biodiversity loss
         Conclusion:
         ...

   Research agent archituecture :

      User Query
      ↓
      Planner
      ↓
      Topic Decomposition
      ↓
      Research Loop
      ↓
      Tool Calls (web search)
      ↓
      Analysis
      ↓
      Final Report
